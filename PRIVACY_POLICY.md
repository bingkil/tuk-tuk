# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-20

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tuk Tuk mobile application ("Tuk Tuk", "the App"),
provided by Bingkil ("we", "us", "our"), handles data.

## Summary

Tuk Tuk does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. There is no account
system, no analytics, no ads, and no network backend. All data stays on your device.

## What the App Accesses, and Why

| Permission / Data | Why it's needed | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | To record video of you while performing/lip-syncing. | Stays on your device; used only to produce the video you're recording. |
| **Microphone** | To record your voice (and, in ambient mode, any audio playing nearby). | Stays on your device; mixed into your recorded video. |
| **Music/media files you select** | To play back a clip while you record, and to mix it into the exported video. | Copied into the App's private local storage on your device so you can reuse it later; never uploaded anywhere. |
| **Storage / Gallery access** | To let you save the exported video to your device's media gallery, or share it via your device's share sheet. | Written to your device's gallery only when you explicitly tap "Save to Gallery" or "Share". |

The App has no internet/backend requirement for its core functionality — recording, trimming,
composing, and exporting all happen locally using on-device processing (CameraX and Media3).

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No account registration or login
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs
- No location data
- No contacts or other personal information beyond what's described above

## Third-Party Services

Tuk Tuk does not integrate with any third-party service (no cloud storage, no social login, no
ad network). If you choose to play music from another app (e.g. a streaming service) while using
Tuk Tuk's ambient recording mode, that other app's own privacy policy governs your use of it —
Tuk Tuk itself does not access, read, or transmit anything from that other app; it simply
records whatever audio is audible through the microphone at the time.

## Data Retention & Deletion

All recordings, imported music, and exported videos are stored locally in the App's private
storage or your device's media gallery. You control this data directly:

- Deleting the App removes all of its private local storage (recordings, local music library).
- Exported videos saved to your device's gallery are managed like any other photo/video on your
  device and are not affected by uninstalling the App.

## Children's Privacy

Tuk Tuk does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not
collect data at all. The App has no age-gating mechanism since no personal data is processed.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are
posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
